## Recommended learning order

1. Java foundations and OOP
2. LLD fundamentals and SOLID
3. Creational, Structural and Behavioral design patterns
4. LLD case studies
5. HLD fundamentals and distributed systems
6. HLD case studies and scalability

Important distinction: HLD focuses on system components and how they communicate; LLD focuses on classes, interfaces, methods, and object interactions. Creational, Structural, and Behavioral are the three classic categories of design patterns within LLD.
